Amber Citadel

Privacy Policy — effective 2026-10-03

1. Introduction

Welcome to Amber Citadel! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how DO I NEED PLANNING LTD (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with your use of Amber Citadel (“the App”).

2. Information We Collect

The App does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. When you use the App, the following data may be collected automatically, mainly by the advertising service described in section 5:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — for example your device's Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Approximate location — estimated from your device's IP address (not precise GPS location).
  • App interactions — for example app launches, taps, and ad views.
  • Diagnostics — for example app launch time, performance, and stability information.
  • Purchase information — if you buy an item in the App, Google Play sends the App a record of that purchase (order number, the item bought, the time of purchase and a purchase token). We never receive your payment card, bank or billing details.
  • App and device integrity signals — when the App checks that it is genuine, Google Play services send Google information about the App (package name, version, signing certificate and Google Play licence status) and a device attestation (whether the device is a genuine, certified Android device, the status of Google Play Protect, and whether apps that can capture the screen or control the device are running). See the Google Play Integrity section.

3. How We Use Your Information

This data is used to:

  • Show advertising in the App and measure its performance
  • Understand how the App is used and improve it (analytics)
  • Prevent fraud, invalid ad traffic, and abuse, and keep the App secure
  • Provide and maintain the App's basic functionality
  • Deliver and restore the items you buy, and prevent fraudulent or duplicate purchases
  • Verify that the App is genuine, installed from Google Play and running on a genuine device, to protect players against cheating, tampering and fraud

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain collected data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, and it is automatically deleted within 90 days. You may also request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us using the details below, and we will process your request promptly. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own policies (see section 5). Purchase records the App receives are kept on your device only for as long as the App is installed; Google keeps its own payment records under Google's policies (see the In-App Purchases section).

5. Advertising

Amber Citadel displays advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads and to detect fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the data listed in section 2 and shares it with Google. Ads may be personalised based on your advertising ID unless you opt out.

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised advertising is used, through Google's consent message.
  • Opting out: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, at any time in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

6. In-App Purchases

Amber Citadel is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases of virtual coins. All payments are processed by Google Play (Google LLC): your payment details are handled by Google under the Google Payments Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy, and are never shared with us.

  • What we receive: only the purchase record described in the Information We Collect section, which the App uses to add the coins to your balance and to finish purchases that were interrupted. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.
  • Virtual coins have no real-money value, cannot be exchanged for cash and can only be used inside the App. They are stored on your device: uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes them.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policies.
  • Parents: every purchase needs the approval of the Google account holder. You can require a password or approval for purchases in the Google Play app (Settings → Authentication, or Google Family Link).

7. Google Play Integrity

To protect Amber Citadel and its players from tampering, piracy and fraud, the App uses the Google Play Integrity API (Google LLC). When the App runs this check, Google Play services send the information described in the Information We Collect section to Google, which returns a signed verdict about the App and the device. According to Google, this data is encrypted, is not transferred to any third parties and is deleted after a fixed retention period. We use the verdict only to verify that the App and the device are genuine; it is not used for advertising and is not sold. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and the Play Integrity API terms.

8. Third-Party Services

Apart from Google AdMob, we may use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting (for example, to measure app performance and fix bugs). These providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent to personalised advertising. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below, or use your device's ad settings described in section 5.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
